Master Gift Wrapper

Save Christmas before the workshop descends into chaos!

Master Gift Wrapper is a co-op Christmas game set in Santa's workshop. Up to four little elves work as a team to pack toys, wrap them in the right paper, add ribbons and bows, and send them off to the sleigh.

Every shift lasts exactly 3 minutes. Orders start simple and get busier and fancier as the clock runs down. How many presents can your team get out of the door?

    How to play

    1. Check the orders at the top of the screen.
    2. Pack: grab a box 📦 and add the right toy 🧸 ⚽ 🚗.
    3. Wrap: add the right paper, put it on a green table and tap to wrap.
    4. Decorate: add a ribbon or a bow if the order asks for one.
    5. Dispatch: take it to the conveyor belt 🛷.

    +10 for a correct present, +5 more when every decoration is right. You can put things down on any worktop and pass them to each other. On a phone, hold it sideways (landscape) to play.
